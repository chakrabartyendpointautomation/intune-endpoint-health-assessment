# Intune Endpoint Health Assessment

## A practical, fixed-scope review for small organizations

Get a clear picture of how your Microsoft Intune-managed endpoints are doing, what needs attention first, and what to do next. The review turns the tenant's available evidence into a prioritized action plan your IT team can own.

### Good fit

Small organizations using Microsoft Intune that need a baseline review, have recurring device compliance or policy issues, or want an independent view before changing their endpoint setup. The first engagement is intentionally limited to one agreed tenant and a defined device population.

### What is included

- A short kickoff to agree on business context, platforms, device-count band, review period, and exclusions.
- A read-only review of the agreed areas: enrollment and inventory, compliance, configuration and assignments, endpoint security, application deployment, updates, identity dependencies, and operating practices.
- A findings register with evidence references, impact, severity rationale, recommended action, effort, dependencies, owner, and validation step.
- A concise written report and a 45-minute readout with the IT owner.
- A prioritized 30-day action plan.

The review uses the tenant's available Intune reports and configuration views. Microsoft provides reports covering areas such as compliance, configuration, enrollment, updates, security, and applications; report freshness and scope depend on the data and device check-ins available in the tenant. [Microsoft Intune reports](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/intune/device-management/reports/overview) and [monitoring compliance policy results](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/intune/device-security/compliance/monitor-policy).

### Boundaries

Assessment and recommendations only. No policy changes, deployment, incident response, penetration testing, certification, or compliance guarantee is included. Work starts only after written authorization, agreed access, and a signed scope. Prefer read-only roles. Exclude user content, secrets, recovery keys, and unnecessary device-level personal data from evidence. Findings are observations for the agreed scope and review date, not a guarantee that every risk has been found.

### Delivery and fee

Typical delivery: five business days after kickoff and access are ready. Quote a fixed fee after a no-cost 20-minute fit call, using the agreed device-count band, platforms, and review areas. State any extra platform, remediation, or additional workshop as a separate option. Do not offer unlimited scope or promise savings/compliance outcomes. Request 50% to schedule and 50% on delivery, subject to a written statement of work and locally appropriate invoicing terms.

### Call to action

Book a 20-minute fit call. Bring your approximate managed-device count, platforms, main concern, and the person who owns Intune. No tenant access is needed for the call.
